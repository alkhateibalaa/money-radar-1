            Scam red flags

            Walk away the moment you see any of these.

            • "Guaranteed" daily or monthly profits.
            • Paid "VIP signal" groups for forex, crypto or stocks.
            • A fee to get a job, unlock earnings or withdraw your money.
            • Income that comes mainly from recruiting other people.
            • Requests for crypto, gift cards, your bank login or one-time codes.
